About your physiotherapist

Hi, I'm Aisha

I'm a musculoskeletal physiotherapist based in Solihull, treating patients across Solihull and Birmingham. My focus is on building rehab plans that fit real life — whether you're recovering from surgery, managing a long-term joint condition, getting back into sport, or supporting an elderly relative to stay mobile and independent at home.

  • Musculoskeletal (MSK) specialist — trauma, orthopaedics & sports injuries
  • Post-operative rehabilitation — structured recovery plans after surgery
  • Elderly care — mobility, falls prevention and independence at home
  • Acupuncture & sports massage — available alongside your rehab plan
